The SUNEN Saga Begins

SUNEN: Children of the Sun — The Emerging

Enter the universe of SUNEN: Children of the Sun—a sweeping fantasy saga shaped by six peoples, six suns, ancient bloodlines, buried histories, and a world whose past has been fractured by survival.

Beneath the roots of a forgotten world, the descendants of six clans endure without the sky their ancestors once knew. Their histories are fading, old divisions remain, and the truths that shaped their people have become fragments passed from one generation to the next.

The Emerging is the first step into that larger universe. Through Montu and a new generation born beneath the earth, the saga begins to uncover what was lost, what survived, and what may be waiting beyond the only world they have ever known.
