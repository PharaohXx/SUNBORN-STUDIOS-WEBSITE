The SUNEN Saga Begins

SUNEN: Children of the Sun — The Emerging

Enter the universe of SUNEN: Children of the Sun — an epic fantasy saga shaped by six distant suns, ancient bloodlines, inherited memory, and a world whose history has become more legend than truth.

Beneath the roots of Solaris, a generation has grown without the sky their ancestors once knew. Old traditions remain, but their meanings are fading. Stories survive, but few can say where memory ends and myth begins.

At the center is Montu — a child of a buried world carrying questions that have outlived the answers.
