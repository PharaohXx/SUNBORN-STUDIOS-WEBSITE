A History Buried in Darkness

What the Darkness Kept

Before memory fractured, six clans came from six distant paths. Driven beneath the earth by a catastrophe no surviving record dares to name, they were forced into one buried refuge beneath the colossal roots. Old rivalries were swallowed by necessity. Separate peoples learned to drink from the same waters, guard the same children, and endure beneath a ceiling untouched by dawn.

Generations later, the clans remember how to survive—but not why they were sent below. Their histories have faded into fragments, their differences into whispered warnings, and the world above into something forbidden.

The elders say the darkness protects them.

The oldest stories say it is waiting.

The Tellies still glow among the roots, though no one remembers what first gave them light—or why that light has begun to weaken. Some believe the sun abandoned their people. Others believe it was taken from them.

If none of them has ever seen the light, why do they still dream of it?

I · THE STORY

The Saga

SUNEN: Children of the Sun is an epic fantasy saga born from buried memory, inherited strength, and the unbroken will of a people shaped by darkness. Across six clans, a new generation must reclaim the truths taken from them and decide what they will carry forward into the light. Here, those once left beyond the borders of legend stand at its center—not as echoes of another world, but as the makers of their own.

II · THE STUDIO

The Vision

SUNBORN Studios was created to forge worlds where ancient power lives in familiar faces, where forgotten voices become prophecy, and where inheritance is more than blood—it is memory, imagination, and destiny. Through bold fantasy, original mythology, and generations of interconnected stories, the studio illuminates heroes who were never waiting to be discovered. They were always here, carrying worlds within them.
