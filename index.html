What the darkness kept

Six Clans. One Buried World.

They came from six distant lands beneath six different suns, carrying separate bloodlines, customs, wounds, and memories. When ruin descended, the survivors were driven below the earth and forced to become neighbors beneath roots older than their oldest names.

Generations passed without sky. The clans learned to share water, stone, labor, and silence—but survival did not make them one people. Old rivalries slept beside fading histories, and every Bloom carried them farther from the world their elders could no longer fully remember.

Only the Tellies still descend from the unreachable dark above. Their fruit bears a strange living glow, though no one remembers what first fed that light—or why it has begun to change.

In a realm where darkness became inheritance, one child was born carrying something the earth could not bury.

I · The Story

The Saga

SUNEN: Children of the Sun is an epic fantasy saga born from buried memory, inherited strength, and the unbroken will of a people shaped by darkness. Across six clans, a new generation must reclaim the truths taken from them and decide whether the light they seek will save their world—or expose what was hidden beneath it.

II · The Studio

The Vision

SUNBORN Studios was created to forge worlds where ancient power lives in familiar faces, where forgotten voices become prophecy, and where inheritance is more than blood—it is memory, imagination, and destiny. Through bold fantasy, original mythology, and cinematic art, the studio builds legends for those who have waited too long to see themselves at the center of wonder.
