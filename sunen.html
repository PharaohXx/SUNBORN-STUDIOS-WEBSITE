The World of SUNEN

Tyghara Distant lands divided by the Infinite Sea

Tyghara is a vast world of distant lands separated by the Infinite Sea, each shaped beneath the light of its own sun.

Across its horizons are peoples formed beneath different skies, carrying different histories, traditions, and inheritances.

The distances between those lands are so great that from the shores of one realm, the light of another sun cannot be seen.

The story of SUNEN: Children of the Sun begins with Solaris — the first land we explore within Tyghara.

Realm I

Solaris Beneath the Emerald Sun

Solaris rises from the waters of Tyghara beneath an Emerald Sun whose green radiance burns through the golden hues of the atmosphere.

Surrounded on every side by the Infinite Sea, Solaris exists beyond sight of the other distant lands.

For generations, its people lived beneath that emerald light — until survivors from beyond the horizon began reaching its shores.

Beyond the Horizon

The Infinite Sea

Between Solaris and the lands beyond it stretches a dark and immeasurable expanse of water, so vast that the lights of distant suns disappear beyond the horizon.

The Voyage Across the Infinite

The Shores of Solaris

Long before Montu was born beneath the roots, survivors began reaching the Solarian coast after impossible voyages across the Infinite Sea.

They had crossed from worlds that could no longer shelter them.

Families came carrying what remained of their people — memories, customs, children, stories, and the fading inheritance of the suns beneath which they had been born.

They came from Olamar, Kush, Tyghan, Mhalamer, and Volero.

Behind them were homelands they could no longer return to.

Ahead of them stood Solaris — foreign, isolated, and alive beneath a sun that was not their own.

Their arrival was not conquest. It was survival.

The Survivors

Five Peoples Crossed the Sea

The refugees who reached Solaris did not arrive as one people.

Each carried a separate history, shaped beneath a different sun and rooted in a world that had already been lost.

Solaris became the place where those histories were forced to meet.

The Emerging

SUNEN: Children of the Sun

Generations after the refugees reached Solaris, their descendants inherit a world shaped by memories they no longer fully understand.

Montu belongs to that buried generation — children raised beneath Solaris among traditions, stories, and fragments of a history slowly disappearing into shadow.

As the certainty of the world around him begins to fracture, questions surrounding his family, the Oasis, and the world above become impossible to ignore.
