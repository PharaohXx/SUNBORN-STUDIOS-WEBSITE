Realm I

Solaris Beneath the Emerald Sun

Solaris rises from the endless waters of Tyghara, isolated beneath an Emerald Sun and surrounded on every side by the Infinite Sea.

Beyond its shores, dark water stretches so far that no neighboring land can be seen. No foreign sun hangs above the Solarian horizon. From the coast, there is only sea, distance, and the green light of Solaris burning through the golden hues of its atmosphere.

For the people born beneath it, the Emerald Sun was more than something in the sky. Its light belonged to their blood, their history, and the land itself.

But the Solarians would not remain alone.

Beyond the Horizon

The Infinite Sea

Between Solaris and the worlds beyond it lies an expanse of water so vast that the lights of distant suns disappear beyond the horizon.

The Voyage Across the Infinite

The Shores of Solaris

Long before Montu was born beneath the roots, survivors began appearing upon the shores of Solaris.

They had crossed the Infinite Sea from worlds that could no longer shelter them. Families came carrying what remained of their people: memories, customs, children, stories, and the fading inheritance of the suns beneath which they had been born.

They arrived from Olamar, Kush, Tyghan, Mhalamer, and Volero.

Their arrival was not conquest. It was survival.

Behind them were homelands they could no longer return to. Ahead of them stood Solaris — foreign, isolated, and alive beneath a sun that was not their own.

The Survivors

Five Peoples Crossed the Sea

The refugees who reached Solaris did not arrive as one people. They carried separate histories from separate worlds, each shaped beneath a different sun.

Solaris became the place where those histories were forced to meet.

The Light of Solaris

Beneath the Emerald Sun

Across Solaris, green light falls upon a realm washed in the warm golden haze of its atmosphere. The Emerald Sun hangs alone above the land, reflected across the Infinite Sea and woven into the oldest inheritance of the Solarian people.

To those born beneath Solaris, its light was natural. It belonged to the world that had shaped them.

To those who arrived from beneath other suns, that same light was something entirely different.

Generations Later

The World Beneath the Roots

The story of Children of the Sun begins long after those first voyages. The children who inherit that history are born underground, beneath the roots of Solaris, surrounded by traditions whose meanings have begun to fade.

They know the Oasis as home. They know the stories left behind by those who came before them.

But memory has become fragile, and the truth of how their people came to live beneath the earth is disappearing with the generations old enough to remember it.

Among those children is Montu.

The Emerging

SUNEN: Children of the Sun

Born beneath a world he has never seen, Montu belongs to a generation raised among fragments of inherited memory. As old certainties begin to fracture, the questions surrounding his family, the Oasis, and the world above become harder to ignore.

The Emerging begins the SUNEN saga with the children who inherited the last pieces of a history they were never meant to fully understand.
