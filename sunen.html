The Realm

Tyghara

Tyghara stretches across an immense, seemingly endless oceanic world. Its lands are separated by distances so vast that one coast cannot see the light of another land's sun beyond the horizon.

Each realm developed beneath a different sun, shaped over generations by its own light, environment, people, customs, and inherited connection to that sky.

SUNEN does not begin by revealing all of Tyghara at once. The first land we explore is Solaris — the realm whose Emerald Sun still burns above the Infinite Sea.

Realm I

Solaris Beneath the Emerald Sun

Solaris rises from the endless waters of Tyghara, isolated beneath an Emerald Sun and surrounded on every side by the Infinite Sea.

Beyond its shores, dark water stretches so far that no neighboring land can be seen. No foreign sun hangs above the Solarian horizon.

From the coast there is only sea, distance, and green light spilling through the golden hues of the Solarian atmosphere.

For the people born beneath it, the Emerald Sun belongs to the land, their history, and the inheritance carried through generations of Solarian blood.

But Solaris would not remain alone.

Beyond the Horizon

The Infinite Sea

Between Solaris and the worlds beyond it stretches an expanse of dark water so vast that the lights of distant suns disappear beyond the horizon.

The Voyage Across the Infinite

The Shores of Solaris

Long before Montu was born beneath the roots, survivors began reaching the Solarian coast after impossible voyages across the Infinite Sea.

They had crossed from worlds that could no longer shelter them.

Families came carrying what remained of their people — memories, customs, children, stories, and the fading inheritance of the suns beneath which they had been born.

They came from Olamar, Kush, Tyghan, Mhalamer, and Volero.

Behind them were homelands they could no longer return to.

Ahead of them stood Solaris — foreign, isolated, and alive beneath a sun that was not their own.

Their arrival was not conquest. It was survival.

The Survivors

Five Peoples Crossed the Sea

The refugees who reached Solaris did not arrive as one people.

Each carried a separate history, shaped beneath a different sun and rooted in a world that had already been lost.

Solaris became the place where those histories were forced to meet.

The Emerging

SUNEN: Children of the Sun

Generations after the refugees reached Solaris, their descendants inherit a world shaped by memories they no longer fully understand.

Montu belongs to that buried generation — children raised beneath Solaris among traditions, stories, and fragments of a history that is slowly disappearing.

As the certainty of the world around him begins to fracture, questions surrounding his family, the Oasis, and the world above become impossible to ignore.
